# Return Policy (fictional, PoC)

> Fictional, simplified company policy created for the Hardware Service Decision Copilot PoC.
> It is not based on, and does not claim compliance with, any consumer law.
> The decision agent follows these rules for every **return** case. Rule IDs (e.g. `RET-2`) are cited in decision justifications.

## RET-1 Return window
A product may be returned within **14 calendar days** of the purchase date entered in the form (day of purchase = day 0).
- Day 0–14: eligible for return, subject to the remaining rules.
- Day 15 or later: the return is **Rejected**. The customer is told that a complaint (warranty) case may be filed instead if the product is defective.

## RET-2 Resaleable condition
A return is accepted only if the product can be sold again as new. The photo must show **no signs of use**, in particular:
- no scratches, scuffs, dents, cracks or chips on the body or screen,
- no stains, dirt, dust build-up, fingerprints on glossy surfaces visible as residue, or stickers added by the user,
- no missing visible parts (covers, stands, buttons, keys),
- no visible liquid marks or discoloration.

If any sign of use is visible, the return is **Rejected** and the reason names the visible sign.

## RET-3 Photo quality
The photo must show the product named in the form, clearly enough to assess RET-2. If the photo is blurry, too dark, cropped so the product cannot be assessed, shows a different product type, or shows no product, no decision is made (**Needs manual review**) and the customer is asked to file a new case with a better photo.

## RET-4 Uncertain condition
If the photo shows the product but a sign of use cannot be confirmed or ruled out (e.g. a possible reflection vs. a possible scratch), the decision is **Needs manual review**. The agent states what exactly is uncertain.

## RET-5 Reason for return
The customer does not have to give a reason. A given reason does not change the decision, except when it reveals that the product was used or damaged (e.g. "it fell once"), in which case RET-2 applies.

## RET-6 Next steps after acceptance
1. Pack the product in its original packaging with all accessories.
2. Send it within 14 days of this decision to: Hardware Service Returns, ul. Przykładowa 1, 00-001 Warszawa (fictional address).
3. The product is inspected on arrival. The decision becomes final only after this physical inspection.
4. The refund is issued to the original payment method within 14 days of a positive inspection.

## RET-7 Manual review
For **Needs manual review**, the customer is told to contact support at `support@example.com` (fictional) or to file a new case with a better photo, as applicable.
